The Gallery

What survives of Kooba Cola is mostly ink: the advertisements Victor Fox splashed across his comic books in 1940–41, the coupons and catalogs that came with them, and one small piece of stamped tin. The gallery hangs forty-one pieces in nine rooms. Every piece is catalogued by the museum; the newer rooms carry their catalog numbers. One is featured every day on the museum’s social channels. Click any framed piece to view the original scan, unframed.

Fox’s house ads were produced for the Kooba Corporation’s campaign — placed through the Harry A. Berk agency — and ran unsigned, as house ads did. Where an artist is documented, we say so; where none is, the work stands as the campaign’s own.
