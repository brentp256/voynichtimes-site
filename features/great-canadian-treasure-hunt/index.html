Treasure Hunt Profile

The Great Canadian Treasure Hunt

217 gold coins. Six regions. One very large country.

Organized by The Northern Miner (owned by EarthLabs Inc., TSXV: SPOT), The Great Canadian Treasure Hunt launched in 2025 with a simple, audacious premise: hide real gold across Canada, publish the clues, and let tens of thousands of hunters do the rest. On October 2, 2026, the hunt officially entered its second year with a brand-new regional prize — six one-ounce gold coins, valued at over $35,000 CAD, waiting somewhere in Chibougamau, Quebec.

The prizes

The headline number is the Grand Prize: 217 one-ounce gold coins, worth well over $1.2 million CAD, minted from gold produced at Agnico Eagle's Detour Lake Mine. It remains hidden.

Around it sit the regional bonus prizes — six one-ounce gold coins each (roughly $35,000 CAD apiece), hidden one region at a time. As of October 2026, six prizes remain in play:

  • Chibougamau, Quebec — the new year-two prize, tied to IAMGOLD's Nelligan Complex in the Chibougamau-Chapais district
  • Sudbury, Ontario
  • Manitoba
  • Saskatchewan
  • Val d'Or, Quebec
  • The Grand Prize

One regional prize has already been claimed: a six-coin bonus worth over $34,000 CAD was found near Nipissing Falls in Cobalt, Ontario in October 2025 by Simon Fallon and his father.

How it works

The hunt is built around Canada's mining story — part history lesson, part modern exploration showcase. Each regional prize comes with published clues (the Chibougamau clue is live now), and the organizers pair each release with project highlights from the surrounding mining district. The Chibougamau launch, for example, spotlights IAMGOLD's district-scale approach at the Nelligan Complex, where disparate deposits across 1,340 square kilometres have been pulled into a single land package holding over 7 million ounces of indicated and inferred gold.

Tens of thousands of hunters are actively searching. Clues and community updates go out through The Northern Miner's channels — treasure.northernminer.com is the hub for full contest rules, FAQs, and the Chibougamau clue.

The fine print

  • 18+. No purchase necessary.
  • Open to residents of Canada only.
  • All prize valuations in CAD, based on spot gold prices as of January 29, 2026 — they fluctuate with the market.

Who's behind it

The Northern Miner has covered the mining industry since 1915 and now operates alongside MINING.COM, CEO.CA, and the Canadian Mining Journal under EarthLabs Inc. The hunt's sponsor bench is a who's-who of Canadian gold: IAMGOLD Corporation, Agnico Eagle Mines Limited, Sprott Money, Kinross Gold Corporation, the World Gold Council, McEwen Inc., Alamos Gold Inc., Ernst & Young LLP, and Mining Matters.
