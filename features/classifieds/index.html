The Sponsors, On The Record

What the official rules say — and what they don't. A sponsor earns a Yes only when its own rules state the full prize count, or its winners are published where entrants can find them. A dash means the desk has not been able to establish it either way; the desk does not guess.

Every listing in The Classifieds is read against its official rules by the Voynich Times Sweepstakes Desk before it appears — prize, value, deadline, eligibility, and entry frequency as the rules state them, not as the promotion advertises them. Listings are pulled when they close. No listing here is an endorsement of the sponsor, and no sponsor pays to appear. Deadlines are the sponsors' own; enter at the official entry page, and read the rules there before you do.
