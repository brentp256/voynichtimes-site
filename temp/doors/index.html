Jawa — the vinyl-cape one

Kenner's Jawa reached the 12-back card in 1978 wearing a vinyl cape; a cloth cape soon replaced it. The two differ only in the covering on the figure's back — same sculpt, same blaster. Every photograph in the Jawa room is samseventy's.
