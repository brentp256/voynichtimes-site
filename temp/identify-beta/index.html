What have I got?

Upload a photograph of a toy and this test tool compares it against the photographs Tangiblia holds, then suggests the catalog entries it most resembles. Pick a match to open its catalog page and check it against your toy.

This is a beta, and it behaves like one.

It matches only against items whose photographs we hold. This test carries the action-figure photographs in the file — if we hold no photograph of your toy, it cannot find it.

Similar-looking figures will confuse it. A match is a suggestion to check, not a determination.

Results improve as the photo file grows. Photographs are added as sellers grant permission, and every addition sharpens the next search.

Your photograph never leaves your device. The matching runs here, in your browser, against fingerprints of our photographs computed beforehand. Nothing is uploaded and no outside service is called.
